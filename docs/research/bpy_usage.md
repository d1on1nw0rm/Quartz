# Real Blender API usage by add-ons (bpy usage)

> Quartz - author: D1on1n. Report generated by `tools/scan_bpy_usage.py` (static analysis with `ast`, without executing the analyzed code). Raw data in `data/bpy_usage.json`.

## 0. Key findings

- **473 distinct symbols** were found under the tracked roots; **304** of them (64%) appear in a single group — the tail is long, but the core is small.
- In the real packages (add-ons + `bl_operators`, 5,568 references), the 'easy core' set covers **75.3%** of references; Tier 1+2 (89 symbols) cover **78.5%** and Tier 1+2+3 (169 symbols) cover **91.2%**.
- `bpy.types.Operator` is inherited by **12 of 13 add-ons** (405 classes). Registration (`register_class`/`unregister_class`), `bpy.props.*` and `Panel`/`Menu`/`PropertyGroup` form the backbone of virtually every add-on.
- `bpy.ops` is wide and shallow: **98 distinct operators** called, only 9 in 2+ groups. `bpy.ops.object.mode_set` is the exception (Tier 1) — Quartz needs a concept of mode (Object/Edit/Pose) early on.
- The implicit surface weighs as much as the imported one: 78 `context` members and 41 `UILayout` members (`layout.prop` alone adds up to 890 references). Even headless, the shim needs a 'recording' `UILayout` (a no-op that records calls).
- The `bpy.app.translations.pgettext_*` family appears in Tier 1: it is trivial (identity) and should arrive on day one.
- `bmesh` is used directly by only 1 add-on(s) and `gpu`/`blf` by 3; both can stay in Tier 3 / stubs at the start.
- 32 symbols are references to native UI classes (`*_MT_*`, `*_PT_*`, `*_HT_*`), almost always for `append`/`remove` on menus — the shim should provide 'ghost' menus that accept extensions.
- Add-ons with ≤ 8 symbols outside the core (easy wins): `hydra_storm`, `io_anim_bvh`, `io_curve_svg`, `ui_translate`. 7 templates use only the core.
